Where the site's own files live

This project keeps one private record online: your rulings, the brief, the research and the digest of your business files. The website's own files are a small part of it, and they will be public by nature once live. Cloudflare can publish a site straight from a folder of an online repository: you connect it once, and every checked change then reaches your address by itself. guessed from Cloudflare's own pages on its hosting, read today; connecting it has not been tried yet, and is tried with you before the plan is signed.

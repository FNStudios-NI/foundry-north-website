# Lessons — foundry-north-website

Every lesson this project learns, from any work: a run, planning, an exploration, a research round, a job or a session. One inbox for all of them. Why: his ruling 10 of 12 Nov 2026, "one place where everything leaves logs"; a lesson kept in a findings file, a handover or a memory is never read.
Written only by `python tools/record.py --lesson ...`, which stamps each line with the clock and gives it the next number; never by hand. Why: a typed time or number drifts, and two writers at once must never share a number.
One line per lesson, in this form, with ` · seen: <seen>` at its end when given:
`- L<nnn> · <yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm> · <from> · <kind> · <what happened> · file: <file> · "<the exact line>"`

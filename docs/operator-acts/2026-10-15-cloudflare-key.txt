STEP 1 - in the browser, signed in to Cloudflare
Why: this is Cloudflare's own page for making keys.

Open this address:

https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens

Pass: you see a page headed "API Tokens" with a button "Create Token".


STEP 2 - on that page
Why: a custom key lets us give it only the three permissions below.

Press "Create Token". Scroll to "Create Custom Token" at the bottom and press "Get started".

Pass: you see a form with "Token name" and "Permissions".


STEP 3 - the form: name and permissions
Why: each line below is one permission; nothing else is added.

Pass: exactly three permission lines, as above. No line says "DNS | Edit".


STEP 4 - the form: which domain and account
Why: the key reaches this one domain and nothing else.

STEP 5 - the bottom of the form
Why: Cloudflare shows a summary before making the key.

Press "Continue to summary", check it matches the three lines and the one domain, then press "Create Token".

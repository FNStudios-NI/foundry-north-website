COUNT YOUR VISITORS, AND TELL GOOGLE YOUR SITE EXISTS  -  your act, about 30 minutes, in a browser (phone or laptop)
==========================================================================

What it unlocks: you can see how many people visit and where they came from; Google starts showing your site in search and on Maps.
Why you and not me: these need your Cloudflare and Google sign-ins, and changing what my key may do is always yours (your "approve all", 16 Oct).

Nothing here changes how the site looks. Nothing here costs money. There is nothing to type into a terminal.
Do the four parts in order; Part C needs me for a moment in the middle.


PART A - let my key read the visitor numbers (about 3 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - in the browser, signed in to Cloudflare
Why: this is where your keys live.

Open: https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens

Pass: you see "foundry-north-website master" in the list.


STEP 2 - on that page
Why: we add two "read only" lines to the key you already made. The key itself stays the same; nothing to copy or save.

Press the three dots beside "foundry-north-website master", then "Edit".
Press "+ Add more" twice and fill in the two new lines:
  Zone      |  Analytics          |  Read
  Account   |  Account Analytics  |  Read
Leave every other line as it is.

Pass: the permissions list now has those two lines added, and still shows "DNS | Edit".


STEP 3 - the bottom of the form
Why: Cloudflare shows a summary before saving.

Press "Continue to summary", then "Update token".

Pass: you are back on the API Tokens page with no error.


PART B - switch on the visitor counter (about 2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 - in Cloudflare
Why: Cloudflare's own counter is free and sets no cookies, so the site still needs no cookie banner.

Go to Workers & Pages (in the left menu), press "foundry-north-site", then the "Metrics" tab.
Under "Web Analytics", press "Enable".

Pass: Web Analytics shows as enabled.

Then tell me "counting on". I re-publish the same site once so the counter starts (Cloudflare only adds it on the next publish).


PART C - Google Search Console (about 5 minutes)
------------------------------------------------
This is Google's free tool for site owners: it shows which searches bring people to you, and tells Google to read your site now.

STEP 5 - in the browser, signed in to the Google account you use for the business
Why: the account you use here is the one that sees the reports.

Open: https://search.google.com/search-console

Pass: you see "Select property type" or a page with "Add property" in the top-left menu (if so, press it).


STEP 6 - choose the left box, "Domain"
Why: "Domain" covers every address of the site in one go (with and without www).

Type:  foundrynorthstudios.co.uk
Press "Continue".

Pass: you see "Verify domain ownership via DNS record", with a line starting "google-site-verification=".


STEP 7 - copy that line and send it to me in the chat
Why: Google proves you own the domain by finding that line in your domain's settings. I add it through your key. It is not a secret: anyone can read it once it is there.

If Google shows a button to verify through Cloudflare instead, that works too: press it, follow it, and skip to STEP 8's Pass line.

Press "Copy" beside the line, and paste it to me. Leave the Google page open. Do NOT press "Verify" yet.

Pass: I answer "row added".


STEP 8 - back on the Google page
Why: Google now looks for the row.

Press "Verify".

Pass: you see "Ownership verified". (If it says it could not find it, wait 10 minutes and press Verify again; if it still fails, tell me what it says.)


STEP 9 - set my key back to reading DNS only
Why: I needed to edit DNS for going live and for Google's row; after this I do not. A key that can do less is safer.

Open https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens, three dots beside "foundry-north-website master", "Edit".
Change the line  Zone | DNS | Edit  to  Zone | DNS | Read.  Then "Continue to summary", "Update token".

Pass: back on the API Tokens page with no error. Tell me "key back to read".


PART D - Google Business Profile (about 15 to 20 minutes, then Google checks you over a few days)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the panel people see on Google Search and Maps. For a small studio it is often the biggest single source of searches.

STEP 10 - in the browser, the same Google account
Why: only the owner can claim the business.

Open: https://business.google.com/create

Pass: Google asks for your business name.


STEP 11 - fill in the form as Google asks
Why: each answer decides which searches you show up for.

  Business name:   Foundry North
  Category:        Video production service  (type "video production" and pick it from the list)
  A location customers can visit?  Choose "No" if you work at your clients' places and not from a public studio. Then add the towns or regions you serve.
  Phone and website:  https://foundrynorthstudios.co.uk

Pass: Google reaches a page about verifying your business.


STEP 12 - verify
Why: Google checks that the business is real before it shows the profile.

Choose whichever way Google offers (often a short video of your workplace, sometimes a phone call or a postcard).

Pass: Google says your profile is being reviewed, or that it is verified.


When you have finished all four parts, tell me "done", plus anything Google asked that you weren't sure about.
If anything looks different from a Pass line, stop and tell me what it says.

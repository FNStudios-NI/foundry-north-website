The team

I stay with you on the brief and the design. I run the plan, merge work once it's been checked, and never build. Under me, I'd set up three desks. Each owns one part of the morning above, and each can work at the same time as the others without touching the same files.

Owns the grey steps

scout

Everything that runs each morning on the home server: the collectors, the cleaner, the judge, the store of jobs, and the email and notification senders. It hands the other desks one fixed thing: today's jobs, with scores and reasons, in a shape that doesn't change without your say.

Owns the writing

packs

The master CV, every tailored CV and cover letter, the check that nothing is invented, and the one true line. It hands the others a pack (CV and letter as PDF and Word) for any job. Its first job is the refreshed master CV.

Owns what the user sees

app

The UI and UX desk you asked for. It owns the page, from design, look, motion and sound to the home-screen icon, sign-in and how notifications appear. The four research pages move here. It builds against the other two desks' fixed shapes, using stand-in data, so it can start before they finish.

Why packs isn't part of scout. Scout is plumbing: it's judged by whether it ran at 6:30 and found real jobs. Packs is writing: it's judged by reading the result and asking whether it would get the user an interview. They need different checks and they fail in different ways. And the packs desk can start on the CV today, before scout exists.

Desk or short-lived helper?

DESK
For work that lasts longer than one job, has to remember what's been tried and what broke, owns something others build on, and needs looking after once it's live. Job sites change and collectors break. Packs get better as replies come in. The design keeps evolving. A desk keeps its own instructions, its own mailbox from me, and plans its own work.
HELPER
For one bounded job that ends in a file and needs no memory: today's four research pages, reading what the home server runs, surveying which local job sites allow programs, checking a worker's result, pricing what the AI will cost. Helpers are cheap, start fresh and can't drift. A whole desk for a one-off job is dead weight.
2. Which team?

My recommendationa. Each desk has a job no other desk has, which is the test for giving it a folder. Three desks plus one checker also fits your cap of four agents at once.

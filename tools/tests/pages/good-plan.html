How it works

Think of it as a darkroom with a contact sheet. A scan goes in, gets a name and a date guess, and lands on one page of thumbnails you can mark.

How big each piece is

Small, medium or large. A build day is one worker for one day. The biggest unknown is how faded the oldest prints are.

Budget and hard stop

Budget
10% of your week. When it is spent, the piece in hand finishes and nothing new starts.
Hard stop
The planned end plus 50%: about 4½ running hours from go.

These are the defaults. To change either, write the new number in a note when you sign; otherwise they stand.
